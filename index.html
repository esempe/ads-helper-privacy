ads helper — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

ads helper is an internal tool used by its operator to manage advertising campaigns in Meta ad accounts through the Meta Marketing API. It is not offered to the general public.

Information we access

When a user authorizes ads helper through Facebook Login, the tool receives an access token and may read the user's public profile (name and ID), the list of ad accounts, Pages and business portfolios the user manages, and campaign, ad set, ad, creative and performance data of those ad accounts. The tool can also create and modify campaigns, ad sets, ads and creatives in those ad accounts on the user's behalf.

How we use it

The data is used only to display and manage the user's own advertising campaigns. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising to the user.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on infrastructure controlled by the operator and are used solely to call the Meta Marketing API. Campaign data is processed transiently and kept only as request logs for troubleshooting.

Data deletion

A user can revoke the tool's access at any time in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites. Revoking access invalidates the stored token. To request deletion of any remaining data, contact the operator at the e-mail below; requests are fulfilled within 30 days.
